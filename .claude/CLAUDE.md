# CLAUDE.md — Owl

## Project overview

See the concept doc of the project in [`docs/concept.md`](docs/concept.md).

## Related projects

- [Nightingale](https://github.com/weibeld/nightingale): information retrieved from Owl may be used as additional input for the story authoring process in Nightingale. There is no direct connection between the two projects, such as, for example, Nightingale automatically retrieving information from Owl, rather, Nightingale story authors may just manually do research in Owl and extract information for their Nightingale stories.
- [owl-data](https://github.com/weibeld/owl-data) (temporary): the data currently collected in owl-data, while Owl does not yet exist, will eventually be imported into Owl as the initial data set and the owl-data project will be deleted.
